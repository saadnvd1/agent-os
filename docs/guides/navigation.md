# Getting around

## Where you left off

Every chat remembers where you were reading. Open it again and you land there,
not at the bottom. The place is kept on the server, so it follows you to your
phone.

- If the chat has something new since you last looked, it opens at the first new
  message, with a **New** line above it.
- If nothing is new, it opens where you stopped.
- If you were at the end, or never opened the chat, it opens at the latest.
- A notification, **Needs you** and the Inbox always open at the latest.

Scrolling only counts once you do it yourself, so a chat that was loading never
overwrites your place.

## Back and forward

Switching chats, or opening a bookmark or a search hit, adds a step to your
browser history. Jumping around inside a chat does not. So **Back** means the
chat or bookmark you came from, and it returns you to the spot you were at.

- The arrows next to the sidebar toggle (desktop) and beside the menu (phone) go
  back and forward. Right-click, or hold on a phone, for the list of chats they
  would pass.
- The browser's own keys work too, and AgentOS binds none of them: ⌘[ and ⌘]
  or ⌘← and ⌘→ on a Mac, Alt+← and Alt+→ elsewhere, a mouse's side buttons, and
  the swipe from the edge on iPhone and iPad.
