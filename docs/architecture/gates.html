Merge gates and review

Nothing merges unless six gates pass on the exact commit being merged.

The six gates

From open PR to merged

Arm, sign off, land

Key states

pass
Ready on this commit.
wait
Not yet: CI running or settling, no review of this commit yet.
fail
Counted against the task, once per commit.
escalate
Goes straight to you as an ask.

In plain words

External pull requests in a workspace's repository get the same gates except stack, and only when they aren't from a fork.

With merge approvals on, sensitive or large PRs also wait for your yes on that commit. A new commit needs a new yes.
