Sharing

A read-only snapshot link to a chat or a page, checked and masked on your machine before anything leaves it.

Making a link

What the reader can do

Key states

active
The link opens.
expired
Answers expired or revoked; the page is deleted.
revoked
Removed by you in Settings → Shared links; the page is deleted at once.

In plain words

Public sites are not masked: a link to a public repository stays as it is. Only names that identify your machines and network are hidden. A share is capped at 5 MB, page and Markdown together.

The link is the only secret. Anyone who has it can read the snapshot, so share it as you would share its contents.
