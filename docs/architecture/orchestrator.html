The orchestrator

One standing chat per workspace that starts tasks, answers blocked agents and merges through the gates, and brings you what is yours to decide.

Key states

event: fresh
Seen once; a condition is armed when the next look still sees it.
event: delivered
Sent to the chat.
event: cleared
The condition went away. It can't re-fire for 15 minutes.
ask: open
Waiting on you.
ask: answered
Approved, declined or replied to.

In plain words

The orchestrator is a chat like any other, made the first time you open a workspace's section. Its tools are served to it in-process and checked for the workspace; a call made while AgentOS restarts waits for it to return.

Recipes, schedules and reminders are how it starts work later: see Schedules and recipes.
