Workspaces and cross-orchestrator messages

A workspace groups projects under one orchestrator, finishes work cleanly, and keeps a log of what merged.

What a workspace holds

Done, archive and restore

A log for every merge

Between orchestrators

Key states

running
A session or task in use.
done
Finished; sits on the Done shelf.
archived
Out of the sidebar, the needs-you count and the orchestrator's view; never deleted.
dropped
Rejected; the PR is closed. It can be restored.

In plain words

Deleting a workspace keeps its projects. A project belongs to at most one workspace, and the orchestrator can add a repository on the machine as a project of its own workspace.

Clean up idle sessions shows what will happen to each first and never merges: open PRs are listed for their own Done.
