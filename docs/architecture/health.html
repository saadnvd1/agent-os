Health detectors and watchers

Cheap checks catch what is going wrong. A short model call judges each trip and says it once. Nothing is fixed automatically.

From a trip to a line in the chat

The detectors

The machine load that feeds the queue

Key states

open
Tripped and still true.
closed
Cleared for 5 minutes.
unjudged
Tripped but the watcher was skipped by a usage brake.
dismissed
Muted for that check and subject for 24 hours.

In plain words

Report only: nothing is paused, stopped or restarted by a detector or a watcher. Thresholds and the notes that tell the watcher what normal looks like are in Settings → Health. Rows are never deleted.

A turn that has produced nothing for 8 minutes shows as Stalled and sends its orchestrator one event; that is configurable per workspace and lives in the chat, not here.

Key files

  • lib/health/detectors.ts the checks
  • lib/health/watchers.ts the judge calls
  • lib/health/tick.ts when they run
  • lib/health/store.ts anomaly rows
  • lib/load/monitor.ts load sampling
  • lib/load/level.ts green, amber and red
  • lib/load/start-gate.ts the gate the queue reads
